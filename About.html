Our Story

RoleForgeAI was built out of frustration. Most resume builders are either too expensive, cluttered with unnecessary features, or simply not designed for Indian students applying to tech companies and law firms.

We noticed that law students especially had no dedicated tool — every resume builder assumed you were a software engineer. So we built a tool that understands both worlds — the GitHub-and-LeetCode world of tech, and the Moot-Court-and-PIL world of law.

Everything runs in your browser. Your data is yours. It always will be.
