Common Questions
Is RoleForgeAI free to use?
Building and previewing your resume is completely free. You can download your resume with a RoleForgeAI watermark at no cost. To remove the watermark and get a clean professional PDF, there is a one-time payment of ₹49 — less than a cup of coffee.
Is my resume data saved anywhere?
No. All data you enter is processed entirely in your browser and is never sent to any server. Refreshing the page will clear your data, so download your resume before closing.
How do I download my resume as PDF?
Fill your details → click "Generate Resume" → click "Print / Save PDF" → in the print dialog select "Save as PDF". Works best in Google Chrome.
Why is there a Law & Legal domain?
Most resume builders only cater to tech roles. We built dedicated law tracks — Moot Courts, PILs, BigLaw keywords — because law students deserve a proper ATS tool too.
