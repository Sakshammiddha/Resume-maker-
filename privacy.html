Privacy Policy
Last updated: July 2026  |  Effective date: July 2026

RoleForgeAI is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. By using our website at roleforgeai.github.io/Resume-maker-/, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

RoleForgeAI is a client-side resume building tool. This means:

  • All resume data you enter (name, email, experience, education, skills) is processed entirely in your browser
  • We do NOT store, transmit, or save any personal resume data on our servers
  • No account or registration is required to use our tool
  • Your resume data is never sent to any external server by us

We may collect the following non-personal information automatically:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device type (mobile/desktop)
  • Pages visited and time spent on site
  • Referring website
  • General geographic location (country/city level only)
2. How We Use Information

Non-personal analytics data is used solely to:

  • Understand how users interact with RoleForgeAI
  • Improve the tool's features and usability
  • Monitor website performance and fix technical issues

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Google AdSense & Advertising

RoleForgeAI uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense may use cookies and similar technologies to show you relevant ads based on your browsing history and interests.

  • Google may use the DoubleClick cookie to serve ads based on your visit to our site and other sites
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings
  • You can also opt out via Network Advertising Initiative

We do not control what ads Google displays. Google's use of advertising cookies is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Cookies

RoleForgeAI itself does not set any first-party cookies. However, third-party services we use (such as Google AdSense and Google Analytics) may set cookies on your device.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some features.

5. Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand website traffic patterns. Google Analytics collects anonymous data about your visits. This data is aggregated and does not personally identify you. You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

RoleForgeAI is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at roleforgeai@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

7. Data Security

Since all resume data is processed locally in your browser and never transmitted to our servers, your resume information is inherently secure. We implement standard web security practices for our website infrastructure.

Our website may contain links to external websites (LinkedIn, GitHub, etc.). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites and encourage you to read their privacy policies.
